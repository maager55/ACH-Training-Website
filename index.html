Level 3 Program Welcome

Welcome to Level 3 of the ACH training program. By now you have worked through the foundations of veterinary nursing and demonstrated the dependability, aptitude, and work ethic required for this next level.

You will be moving into the surgery suite, where the stakes are higher and the patient depends entirely on the surgical team. By the end of Level 3, you should feel confident managing patients under general anesthesia across abdominal, ophthalmic, and orthopedic procedures.

Anesthetic Hypothermia & Active Warming

Anesthetic drugs impair thermoregulation. Hypothermia decreases drug clearance, prolongs recovery, and increases infection risk.

NEVER USE IMPROVISED HEAT SOURCES:
Hot water bottles, microwaved rice bags, latex gloves with hot water, human heating pads, and heat lamps cause severe, delayed tissue burns. Use only approved forced-air warmers or circulating water blankets.
