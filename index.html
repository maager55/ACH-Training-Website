Anesthetic Monitoring Targets

Parameter Dog Normals Cat Normals Notify Doctor If
Heart Rate 60–120 bpm 100–160 bpm Outside range, arrhythmia, pulse deficit
Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) 70–100 mmHg 70–100 mmHg MAP < 70 mmHg (Urgent < 60 mmHg)
SpO2 ≥ 95% ≥ 95% < 95% (Urgent < 90%)
ETCO2 35–45 mmHg 35–45 mmHg < 30 or > 50 mmHg

Quick Formulas

  • Drug Volume (mL): [Weight (kg) × Dose (mg/kg)] ÷ Concentration (mg/mL)
  • Fluid Rate (Anesthesia): Dogs = 5 mL/kg/hr | Cats = 3 mL/kg/hr
  • Reservoir Bag Size: Weight (kg) × 60 mL/kg (Round up to nearest 0.5L, 1L, 2L, 3L, 5L)
